Privacy Policy (English)

1. Use of YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data accessed

  • The name and ID of the YouTube channel the user connects, and the processing and privacy status of videos uploaded by this application.
  • OAuth scopes used: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube (set thumbnails, manage playlists and privacy status).
  • No data of other users or channels, and no comments, subscriptions or watch history, are accessed.

3. Data stored and where

  • One Google OAuth refresh token, a log of the IDs, titles and upload times of videos uploaded by this application, and the connected channel name.
  • Everything is stored only on the operator's own computer and is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Neither the application nor this page uses cookies or visitor tracking.

4. Sharing with third parties

Data is never shared with or sold to third parties, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

5. Deletion and revoking access

  • Choosing "Disconnect" in the application deletes the stored token.
  • You can revoke this application's access at any time via the Google security settings page.
  • The local upload log can be deleted by the operator at any time.

6. Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date.
